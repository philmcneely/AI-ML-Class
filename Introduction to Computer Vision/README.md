Overview
In this module on Computer Vision, we will talk about how advancements in the area of deep learning have enabled our computer systems to see, process, and take decisions on visuals. These developments have led to some very important implementations in the fields of medicine, education, surveillance, and many more. The rise in data and computing power has driven tremendous developments in the machine learning space, and the need to access and process large data is turning out to be increasingly significant with regard to solving real-life/business problems. Currently, deep neural networks can learn the complex relations among the data and provide us with meaningful insights.

The era of Deep Neural Networks has brought up Computer Vision and several use-cases of Computer Vision. It is used in several domains like Public Health, Agriculture, Retail, Automotive Industry, and Fitness for solving complex problems like Object recognition and classification, Human Pose Estimation, Customer Behaviour tracking, etc.

Course Objectives 
After completing this course, you will be able to:

Understand images as data and the steps in working with image data.
Understand the differences between ANNs and CNNs.
Understand and build CNNs for classification tasks.

Project
A hands-on project is to be submitted at the end of the course that will help you to apply convolutional neural networks.


The agricultural industry requires modernization due to the extensive manual work involved in monitoring plant growth. Despite advancements in agricultural technology, the sorting and recognition of plants and weeds remain time-consuming and labour-intensive. Artificial Intelligence (AI) presents an opportunity to revolutionize this industry by reducing the need for manual labour. By utilizing AI and Deep Learning, the identification of plant seedlings can be significantly accelerated, surpassing the efficiency of human labour. This advancement has the potential to improve crop yields, enable human involvement in higher-level decision-making, and promote sustainable environmental practices in agriculture.

 

Develop a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model that can accurately classify the plant seedlings into their respective categories among the 12 species.
