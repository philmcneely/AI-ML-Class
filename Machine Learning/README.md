Consider the following common business questions:

What is the relationship between the sales of a company and the amount spent on advertising?
Can we predict if a customer is going to default on the loan or not based on factors like age, duration of the loan, etc.?
Is email spam or not spam?
Can I group my customers or products into different groups to get the best return on my marketing investment?
Such questions can be answered by training a model using data with known and unknown outcomes, and this takes us into the realm of Machine Learning.

 

Overview
With data now leading the fourth industrial revolution and the world generating more and more of it every second, it is an important skill to be able to handle data well and know techniques to be able to learn from historical data. The Machine Learning course aims to help learners understand how to build models to capture the relationships between variables and a known outcome (that can be continuous or categorical in nature), how to identify patterns in data that are not labeled, and how to gain business insights from the model.

 

Course Objectives
After completing this course, you will be able to:

use linear models to make estimates and forecasts
used non-linear models to perform classification tasks
identify factors that will help drive business outcomes based on models built
observe the similarities and dissimilarities between data points by identifying patterns and grouping them based on different attributes of the business

Project
A hands-on project is to be submitted at the end of the course that would help you to apply the Logistic Regression and Decision Tree algorithms.

The project is about a Personal Loan Campaign for AllLife Bank in the US, which has a growing customer base. The goal here is to analyze the data provided to determine which factors have a significant impact on loan approval. Moreover, it is required to build a predictive model that will help the marketing department to identify the potential customers who have a higher probability of purchasing the loan.
