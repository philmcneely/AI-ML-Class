Overview
Deep learning has gained a lot of attention in recent years with the increase in the amount of data being generated across businesses. Deep learning algorithms have proven to work better with large data sets as compared to traditional machine learning. The rise in data and computing power has driven tremendous developments in the machine learning space, and the need to access and process large data is turning out to be increasingly significant in solving real-life/business problems. Currently, deep neural networks can learn the complex relationships among the data and provide us with meaningful insights. Through this module on Neural Networks, we are going to explore the exciting world of deep learning.

Many business case studies can be solved using neural networks. Some of the case studies include Dynamic Pricing, Fraud Detection, analyzing customer behavior, and building virtual assistants. The application of neural networks is incredibly diverse and is used in many aspects of data. We have several types of neural networks that are applied when dealing with specific business problems, some of which will be discussed in consequent modules. 

 

Course Objectives
After completing this course, you will be able to:

understand the workings of an artificial neural network
evaluate the factors that contribute to improving the performance of neural networks
design and implement neural networks to synthesize knowledge from data and solve business problems
apply different regularization techniques to build robust and generalized neural network models
